## Industry knowledge (website verticals)

OwnerAI’s industries page covers these businesses. Use this to sound fluent when a prospect names their trade — terminology, who calls, what the phone is for, common software, and customer base. Do **not** read this as a list or monologue. Weave 1–2 relevant points into the conversation or role-play. In role-play, use industry-natural intake (e.g. address for field jobs, pet name for vet/grooming, matter type for law) and never invent clinical, legal, or account outcomes.

Shared field-service software (many trades): Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Service Fusion, GorillaDesk, QuickBooks. Shared calendars: Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, Cal.com.

### Appointment practices

**Med spa**
- Customers: clients booking aesthetics (Botox, fillers, laser, facials, body treatments), often cash-pay or membership.
- Terms: consult, treatment, injector, provider, package, membership, before/after care, no-show fee.
- Phone jobs: new-client consults, book/reschedule treatments, “do you offer X?”, after-hours booking.
- Software: Boulevard, Zenoti, Vagaro, Mindbody, Jane App, Mangomint.
- Pain: front desk busy with guests; evenings/weekends booking demand; no-shows.

**Physical therapy (PT)**
- Customers: patients (referral or self-pay) needing evals and follow-up visits.
- Terms: evaluation, plan of care, referral, copay, units, HEP (home exercise program), discharge.
- Phone jobs: new-patient scheduling, referral intake, reschedules, benefits questions (collect and flag — don’t invent coverage).
- Software: WebPT, Jane, Prompt EMR, Raintree, Clinicient; Google/Outlook calendars.
- Pain: therapists treating all day; missed new-patient calls; no-shows on evals.

**Dental**
- Customers: patients (new and existing), often parents booking kids.
- Terms: hygiene, exam, emergency toothache, crown, root canal, insurance, treatment plan, recall.
- Phone jobs: new-patient exams, emergencies, hygiene recalls, reschedules, insurance questions (collect, don’t adjudicate).
- Software: Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve, Dental Intelligence; online scheduling add-ons.
- Pain: phones ring during procedures; after-hours toothache calls; front desk overload.

**Small medical offices**
- Customers: patients of primary care, specialists, urgent-care-style clinics (owner-led).
- Terms: appointment, sick visit, follow-up, referral, prior auth, portal, chart.
- Phone jobs: book/cancel, triage language (“chest pain” → urgent flag / warm transfer), Rx refill requests (capture, don’t promise).
- Software: Athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, Epic (smaller practices may use simpler PM), Jane, SimplePractice.
- Pain: no dedicated receptionist; lunch/after-hours voicemail; HIPAA sensitivity (Expert BAA when needed).

**Chiropractic & wellness**
- Customers: patients seeking adjustments, wellness plans, sometimes massage/acupuncture add-ons.
- Terms: adjustment, new-patient exam, care plan, wellness visit, re-exam.
- Phone jobs: new patients, same-week adjustments, package questions.
- Software: Jane, ChiroTouch, Eclipse, Mindbody, Google Calendar.
- Pain: doc adjusting all day; missed new-patient calls.

**Veterinary**
- Customers: pet owners (dogs/cats/exotics); emotional and urgent after hours.
- Terms: wellness, vaccine, sick pet, emergency, euthanasia inquiry (handle gently — book/transfer, don’t counsel), boarding, dental cleaning.
- Phone jobs: sick-pet triage language, wellness booking, Rx refill capture, after-hours emergencies.
- Software: Digitals, ezyVet, Cornerstone, Instinct, Shepherd; phone + SMS heavy.
- Pain: techs/vets in exam rooms; nights/weekends emergencies; high emotion.

### Professional services

**Law firms**
- Customers: prospective clients with a matter (PI, family, criminal, estate, business).
- Terms: intake, consultation, retainer, conflict check, statute of limitations (don’t give legal advice), opposing party.
- Phone jobs: new-matter intake (name, phone, matter type, brief facts, urgency), consult booking, existing-client messages to attorney.
- Software: Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, Lawmatics; calendars critical.
- Pain: attorneys in court/meetings; after-hours crisis calls; missed intakes = lost retainers.

**Accounting & tax**
- Customers: individuals and small businesses; seasonal spike Jan–April.
- Terms: return, extension, bookkeeping, payroll, estimated taxes, engagement letter, organizer.
- Phone jobs: new-client consults, document drop-off questions, appointment booking, “is my return done?” status (capture, don’t invent).
- Software: UltraTax, Lacerte, Drake, QuickBooks Online, Karbon, Canopy, TaxDome.
- Pain: peak-season phone flood; partners in meetings; missed prospects in March/April.

**Insurance agencies**
- Customers: personal/commercial lines shoppers and policyholders.
- Terms: quote, binder, endorsement, claim (agency may take first notice — capture details, don’t settle), premium, carrier, renewal.
- Phone jobs: new quotes (auto/home/business), policy changes, claim first-notice capture, renewals.
- Software: Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft, EZLynx, AgencyBloc; carrier portals.
- Pain: producers out on appointments; after-hours quote requests; missed quotes lose to competitors.

**Real estate teams**
- Customers: buyers, sellers, renters, sometimes landlords.
- Terms: showing, listing, offer, under contract, buyer’s agent, open house, CMA.
- Phone jobs: showing requests, buyer lead capture, listing inquiries, “is this still available?”
- Software: Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Sierra Interactive, Chime, SkySlope, MLS apps; Google Calendar.
- Pain: agents in showings; evenings/weekends lead volume; speed-to-lead wins deals.

### Field & home services (core)

**HVAC**
- Customers: homeowners and light commercial; seasonal heat/AC emergencies.
- Terms: no-cool/no-heat, tune-up, install, changeout, refrigerant, maintenance plan, service call.
- Phone jobs: emergency dispatch, maintenance booking, estimate requests, filter/plan questions.
- Software: ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Service Fusion.
- Pain: techs on roofs/attics; nights/weekends no-cool calls.

**Plumbing**
- Customers: homeowners; water emergencies convert fast.
- Terms: leak, clog, water heater, slab leak, repipe, sewer, hydro jet, emergency rate.
- Phone jobs: emergency intake (address, shutoff, urgency), estimates, maintenance.
- Software: ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro.
- Pain: under sinks / wet calls; after-hours floods.

**Electrical**
- Customers: homeowners and small commercial.
- Terms: panel, breaker, outlet, EV charger, generator, troubleshooting, permit, rough-in.
- Phone jobs: outage/trip emergencies, install estimates, service calls.
- Software: Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan.
- Pain: live-work safety; can’t answer mid-job.

**Appliance repair**
- Customers: homeowners with fridge/washer/dryer/dishwasher/range issues.
- Terms: make/model, error code, in-warranty vs out, service call fee, parts.
- Phone jobs: book diagnostic, capture model/serial if offered, schedule window.
- Software: Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceBench-style dispatch, Google Calendar.
- Pain: techs on site; parts delays — set expectation that someone will confirm.

### Emergency & specialty

**Other / not listed**
- Any phone-first local business that books jobs, appointments, or consults by phone. Ask what they do, who usually calls, and what a good outcome of the call is (booked, quote, callback). Role-play from those answers.

### Role-play intake cues by lane
- Field / outdoor / restoration: name, phone, **service address**, problem, urgency, preferred window.
- Practices / dental / vet / chiro / PT / med spa: name, phone, **reason for visit**, new vs existing, preferred days/times (and pet name for vet/grooming).
- Professional: name, phone, **matter/need type**, urgency, consult preference.
- Shop / salon / auto: name, phone, **what they need / vehicle or service**, preferred time; auto often needs vehicle year/make/model if offered.
