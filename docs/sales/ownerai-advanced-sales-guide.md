## 5. The problem (talk track)

Most shops already “handle the phone” somehow — and still lose jobs.

**Pain points to surface:**

- Missed calls while on a roof / in a crawlspace / driving.
- After-hours and weekend voicemail that never gets returned.
- A part-time person who can’t cover nights, lunch, or vacation.
- Spam and solicitor calls that waste time (and sometimes get billed by old answering services).

**Useful market framing (from marketing):**

- A large share of calls go unanswered or to voicemail; many callers hang up rather than leave a message.
- A full-time receptionist often runs **$2,800–$4,500+/month** for ~40 hours — and still can’t cover 24/7 or simultaneous rings.
- Traditional answering services often charge **$2–$5 per minute** and may bill spam.
- Cheap DIY AI tools put the setup and babysitting back on the owner.

**ROI line that closes:** If OwnerAI saves **one good job a month**, it usually pays for itself.

## 6. What Advanced is (plain English)

When a customer calls the business:

1. **Answers in seconds**, 24/7/365 — even if three people call at once.
2. **Talks like a trained front desk** for that shop (services, area, hours, FAQs), in **English or Spanish**.
3. **Takes a proper intake** — name, number, address, why they’re calling, urgency.
4. **Screens spam** so junk doesn’t wake the owner or pad the bill.
5. **Texts and/or emails the owner** a clear summary (plus transcript and recording).
6. **Books on the calendar** when the shop uses Google, Outlook, Calendly, or Cal.com.
7. **Sends SMS confirmations / reminders** so jobs don’t go cold.
8. **Warm-transfers emergencies** to the owner’s cell when rules say so.
9. **Scores / qualifies the lead** so the owner knows what matters.
10. Handles **reschedules and cancellations**.
11. OwnerAI runs a **monthly optimization** check-in to tighten the script and results.

**What the customer does:** Keep their existing number. When ready, forward calls to OwnerAI. Instant rollback by turning forwarding off.

## 12. Discovery questions

Ask these early. Write down answers — they become the setup brief.

1. Who answers the phone today when you’re on a job?
2. What happens after 5pm / weekends / holidays?
3. Roughly how many inbound calls per day / week?
4. What’s a typical job worth? Emergency vs estimate?
5. Do you book on a calendar today? Which one?
6. Are you okay with SMS to customers for confirmations?
7. Do you need Spanish often?
8. What would “working” look like in 30 days? (e.g. never miss after-hours leads)
9. Any must-have software later (Jobber, Housecall Pro, etc.)? → park for Expert after Advanced.

## 15. Do / Don’t for new reps

### Do

- Lead with **missed jobs** and the **live demo**.
- Close **Advanced** as the default.
- Confirm call volume, who answers today, and calendar tool.
- Use **one saved job / month** as the value frame.
- Escalate custom / Expert scope to the founder before promising.

### Don’t

- Don’t publish or invent **website prices** that aren’t approved.
- Don’t quote the website’s marketing minute ladder — quote **500 + 500**.
- Don’t promise **Jobber / ServiceTitan / CRM write-back** inside Advanced.
- Don’t lead with AI models, vendors, or technical architecture.
- Don’t discount setup or monthly without founder approval.
- Don’t spend the first meeting designing a custom enterprise contact center.
