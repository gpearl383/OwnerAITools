## 1. Cover / positioning

**One-sentence offer:** OwnerAI Tools is a done-for-you AI receptionist service for small businesses — every call answered 24/7/365, every lead captured, appointments booked, summaries delivered to the owner.

**What we are not:** A DIY $49/mo AI phone app. Customers do not script, train, or babysit an agent. They forward their phone and read their leads.

**Stage truth:** The marketing site, live voice/SMS demo, owner alert pipeline, compliance pages, and internal ops tooling are in production. **MRR today: $0.** The next milestone is first paid clients, not more features.

**Parent company:** CSM Integrated Solutions (csmintegrated.com) provides IT/MSP and broader AI work. OwnerAI is the packaged receptionist product with its own brand, funnel, and pricing.

**Ask (partners):** Warm introductions to owner-operators in plumbing, HVAC, or electrical. Ads ($500/mo) and product/tools ($500/mo) are self-funded for now — **not raising $25–50k** unless that changes later.

## 2. Problem and why now

Phone-dependent businesses lose revenue when humans cannot answer: on a job, with a patient, after hours, or overloaded at the front desk. The next name on Google gets the $4,000 emergency — not the shop that let it ring.

**Substitutes that fail the owner:**

| Alternative | Failure mode |
|-------------|--------------|
| Voicemail | Most callers hang up; no booking, no urgency triage |
| Full-time receptionist | ~$2.8–4.5k/mo payroll burden; still one person, 40 hours, sick days |
| Human answering service | Often $2–5/min, spam billed, rigid scripts, weak handoff |
| DIY AI phone apps (~$49/mo) | Owner must configure, monitor, and debug — the opposite of “done for me” |

**Why now:** Voice AI quality is good enough for intake, booking, and bilingual handling; buyers have heard of AI but do not want another tool to learn; local service demand still runs through the phone.

**Proof narrative already on-brand:** Site and blog math for plumbing (“what a missed 9 PM call costs”) and medical practices (patients lost to voicemail).

## 4. Market and ICP

### Beachhead (90-day lock)

**Home-services owner-operators** — plumbing, HVAC, electrical — multi-truck or growing solo-to-small crews where a single missed emergency call pays for months of service.

**Buyer persona:** Busy owner who lives on the phone. Wants jobs captured and a clean summary/SMS. Will not spend weekends tuning prompts.

**Job to be done:** “Stop losing jobs when I cannot pick up — nights, weekends, and when I’m under a sink.”

### Adjacent (after beachhead proof)

Dental, med spa, and other local practices — already supported in marketing copy and a dedicated blog post. Defer active outbound here until ≥3 home-services clients are live, unless a warm inbound closes itself.

### Anti-ICP

- Buyers shopping purely on lowest monthly price for DIY AI  
- Enterprises wanting custom contact-center platforms (route to CSM)  
- Businesses whose primary channel is not phone/SMS  

### Geography

US-focused; demo and ops currently Long Island / NY-adjacent numbering and Eastern time booking. Expand geography via forwarding — no local office required per market.

**First-customer SKU policy:** Lead with **Advanced** (internal target). Prefer the internal 6-month setup discount over inventing ad-hoc prices. Use Basic only for price-sensitive pilots that still clear capacity. Sell Expert when CRM/HIPAA is a hard requirement — price in the delivery hours.

### Days 31–60 — First revenue and delivery proof

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|

| Proof | Capture before/after (missed-call story, sample alerts, owner quote) |
| Channels | Start or continue $500/mo ads only if setup-call volume from warm/outbound is <4/month |
| Learning | Kill any vertical/script that adds setup hours without raising willingness to pay |

### Days 61–90 — Pattern or pivot

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| Scale target | **3–5 total paying clients** or hit kill criteria below |
| Packaging | Confirm Advanced remains default; adjust only with data |
| Partner packet | Keep §§1, 9, 12 current for intros |
| Next bets | Only then consider dental outbound or light portal/billing work |

### Kill / continue criteria (written)

**Continue / double down if by day 90:**

- ≥3 paying clients, **or**  
- ≥2 paying + ≥25% setup-call → paid close rate on ≥8 setup calls, **and**  
- No delivery disaster (P0 outage unaddressed, or setup >40 hours on Advanced without scope change)

**Kill or hard pivot if by day 90:**

- <1 paying client **and** <10% setup-call → paid on ≥8 setup calls, **or**  
- Willingness to pay clusters only below Basic list with heavy customization, **or**  
- COGS + care hours make Advanced contribution structurally negative

**Pivot options (only if kill triggers):** narrow further (one trade only), raise price and go fully referral, or package a cheaper Basic-only “alerts only” SKU with strict scope — do not pivot to building a DIY self-serve app as the escape hatch.
