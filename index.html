The problem

The phone rings while you're under a sink — and the job walks.

When you don't answer, the next company on Google does. Speed-to-lead is the whole game — and after-hours is where the best jobs hide.

On-the-job rings you can't grab

Hands in a water heater, under a roof, running a crew. The phone lights up, you can't pick up, and that emergency books the next name on Google. You never see the invoice you lost.

Callbacks that die in the notes app

A sticky note, a half-heard voicemail, a "I'll call them back at lunch." By the time you dial, the homeowner already hired someone else — often before you've wiped your hands.

Nights and weekends with no booker

A receptionist covers 40 of 168 hours. Emergencies love the other 128. Without someone who can answer, qualify, and put a job on the calendar overnight, those leads walk — every single night.

How it works

We build it. You forward your phone. That's it.

This is a done-for-you service, not software you have to learn. Most shops go live in about one to four weeks. You never touch a dashboard.

1

45-minute intake with us

Your services, service area, pricing, and how you talk to customers. We script and build your receptionist, test it against real scenarios, and send you a number to call and hear it yourself before anything goes live.

2

Forward your phones

Keep your existing number — nothing changes for your customers. Smart forwarding sends calls always, after-hours only, or only when you don't pick up. Want out? Rollback is instant, any time.

3

Jobs start landing in your inbox

Every call becomes an email: caller info, what they need, full transcript, and the recording. On higher tiers, appointments hit your calendar and contacts hit your CRM automatically. We review and tune it every month.
