Capture

The lead lands in your inbox before they hang up.

No sticky notes. No "someone called about something." Every call becomes a clean summary you can act on between customers.

  • Name, callback number, address, and reason for calling
  • Full transcript plus call recording attached
  • Urgency flags for true emergencies
  • Instant email to the owner (and your team if you want)
  • Monthly lead report so you see what the phone actually produced
  • Nothing dropped when the call is messy or half-finished

Built for the owner, not the CRM power user

You get the facts you need to decide: call back now, book tomorrow, or warm-transfer. Summaries are written for owners who read email between jobs or appointments — not for software admins.

Book

Real calendar slots — not "someone will call you back."

On Advanced and Expert, the receptionist books against live availability so you wake up to a calendar that already has work on it.

  • Live booking with Google, Outlook, Calendly, or Cal.com
  • Respects real availability — no double-booking surprises
  • Handles reschedules and cancellations on the call
  • Coverage-area and request-type qualification before a slot is offered
  • Lead scoring so you know which calls matter most
  • Multi-location / multi-department routing on Expert
