1. Purpose

This guide sells OwnerAI’s initial / core offering: Advanced. Expert / Custom (integrations) is a later conversation — not the first pitch. Keep the public website quote-led; use these numbers on sales calls and proposals.

2. One-sentence pitch

OwnerAI is a done-for-you 24/7 AI receptionist for phone-driven businesses — we build it, tune it, and watch it; you keep your number and forward the phone when you’re ready.

Emphasize managed service, not another DIY app the owner has to babysit.

3. Who we sell to

Beachhead: Phone-first local businesses — owner-operated and small teams that book jobs, appointments, or consults by phone. Four lanes: field & home services (best-proven proof), appointment practices (med spa, PT, dental, small medical), professional services (law, accounting, insurance, real estate), and shop/bay (auto, salons, pet grooming). The main number often rings a cell, the owner’s spouse, or a front desk that’s only there ~40 hours a week. Missed after-hours or busy-day calls lose real revenue.

Buyer wants: Booked work and clean summaries — not another dashboard. Home services remains the strongest demo story; take qualified interest across all four lanes.

5. The problem (talk track)

  • Missed calls on a job, after hours, weekends, or when the part-timer is out
  • Voicemail that never gets returned; spam wasting time (and sometimes billed by answering services)
  • Full-time receptionist often $2,800–$4,500+/mo for ~40 hours — still not 24/7
  • Answering services often $2–$5/min; DIY AI puts babysitting back on the owner

Close line: If OwnerAI saves one good job a month, it usually pays for itself.

6. What Advanced does (plain English)

  1. Answers in seconds, 24/7/365 — even with multiple callers at once
  2. Trained on that shop’s services, area, hours, and FAQs — English or Spanish
  3. Takes proper intake (name, number, address, reason, urgency)
  4. Screens spam so junk doesn’t wake the owner
  5. Emails and/or texts the owner a clear summary (+ transcript and recording)
  6. Books on Google, Outlook, Calendly, or Cal.com when configured
  7. Sends SMS confirmations / reminders so jobs don’t go cold
  8. Warm-transfers emergencies to the owner’s cell per rules
  9. Scores / qualifies leads; handles reschedule / cancel
  10. Monthly optimization with our team

Customer action: Keep their number → forward when ready → turn forwarding off anytime to roll back.
