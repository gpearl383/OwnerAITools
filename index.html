The problem

Most jobs are lost in the first 5 minutes.

When you don't answer, the next company on Google does. Speed-to-lead is the whole game — and after-hours is where the best jobs hide.

Missed calls while you're on a job

Hands in a water heater, under a roof, running a crew. The phone rings, you can't grab it, and that $4,000 emergency calls the next name on Google. It happens every week — you just never see the invoice you lost.

Slow or inconsistent follow-up

Voicemail sits for hours. Answering services take a message you chase down later. By the time you call back, the homeowner already booked someone else — often in the first five minutes.

No reliable booking after hours

A receptionist covers 40 of 168 hours. Emergencies love the other 128. Without a way to answer, qualify, and book overnight, those jobs walk — every single night.
